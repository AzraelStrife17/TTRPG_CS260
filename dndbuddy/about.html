    Dr. Tofu Logged In

    Dungeon and Dragons is an experience that reqiures people to be together, however it is common that people can't be together in the same place. People have circumvented this by playing over voice calls like discord, however without everyone being in the same area there always leaves a chance for one of your friends to fudge the numbers a little bit. Well fear no more deception by your friends because D&D Buddy is here to allow friends from far distances into a D&D experience where every roll is now broadcasted. No more are the days of your friend mysteriously rolling natural 20's when the true reality was they were rolling natural 0's
